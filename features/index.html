Financial Engine

Automated Rent & Advance Reconciliation

Rental arithmetic is messy when handled manually on paper registers. Rentlyo's shared rent calculation engine synchronizes state identically across both apps without rounding drift:

  • Advance Deposit Consumption Mode: Configurable deduction window (Start Month to End Month) allowing seamless transition from holding deposit to consuming it for rent.
  • Partial Payment Carryovers: Tenants who pay split or partial amounts have exact carryovers auto-calculated into the next cycle with automated overdue tracking.
  • Step-Up Escalation Tiers: Pre-program yearly rent increases (e.g., 5% or 10% after month 12) with effective calendar date milestones.
  • Rent Due Day Clamping (1-28): Built-in boundary protection avoids February date overflow bugs and ensures clean monthly cycles.

Utilities & Sub-Meters

Unit-Level Sub-Meter Tariff Management

Sub-meter billing is the single greatest cause of friction in mixed commercial and residential buildings. Rentlyo completely resolves it:

  • Custom Unit Tariffs: Assign commercial tariff rates (e.g. ₹9.50/kWh) to retail shops and residential rates (e.g. ₹7.00/kWh) to flats.
  • Electricity, Water & Gas Types: Support for multiple sub-meter utility types with automatic previous-reading carryforward.
  • Zero Calculation Errors: Enter the current meter reading; the engine automatically computes (Current - Previous) * Tariff and posts to the tenant's ledger.

Regional Accessibility

Complete Hindi & English Bilingual Support

Most property software in India is built exclusively in complex English, making it unusable for local staff, caretakers, and non-English speaking tenants. Rentlyo includes native bilingual support:

  • Every Screen Translated: Rent ledgers, payment reports, PIN login, notices, gate passes, and settings fully localized in Hindi and English.
  • Instant In-App Switcher: Switch languages in real time from settings without restarting the application.
  • Zero Language Barriers: Enables elderly landlords and tier-2 / tier-3 city tenants to navigate balances with absolute clarity.
Security Architecture

Biometric & 6-Digit PIN Security Lockout

Property financial records require bank-grade protection against snooping family members or misplaced phones. Rentlyo implements high-grade local security:

  • 6-Digit PIN & Secondary Biometrics: Fingerprint and Face Unlock support for instant, secure authentication on Android devices.
  • Anti-Brute Force Lockout: Enforces progressive timeouts after repeated invalid PIN entries to prevent unauthorized access.
  • Protected Cloud Database: Granular database security rules ensure tenants can never view other occupants' financial ledgers.
