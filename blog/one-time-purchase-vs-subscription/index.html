One-Time Purchase vs Subscription Property Software: The Real 5-Year Cost Breakdown

By Aaryan Gupta (Software Engineer) • 6 Min Read • Verified Indian SMB Economics

If you manage 5 to 50 rental flats, shops, or hostel rooms in India, you have likely encountered software vendors proposing monthly subscription fees. On the surface, ₹1,499 per month sounds affordable. But what does that subscription actually cost your rental business over 3 to 5 years?

The Compounding Cost of SaaS

Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) relies on recurring revenue. For an independent property owner, this structure creates an ongoing tax on rental cash flow:

  • Year 1: ₹1,499 × 12 = ₹17,988
  • Year 3: ₹1,499 × 36 = ₹53,964
  • Year 5: ₹1,499 × 60 = ₹89,940

Notice what happens if you ever decide to cancel your subscription in year 4: you lose access to your historical payment receipts, tenant logs, and lease records, unless you pay fees to maintain an archive.

The Alternative: One-Time Software Ownership

With Rentlyo's one-time purchase of ₹20,000, your capital expense is paid once. By month 14, you have completely broken even compared to a standard SaaS quote. By year 5, you have saved over ₹69,940 in recurring bills.

Why Can One-Time Software Exist Today?

Historically, software companies had to pay thousands of dollars each month for dedicated web servers and database instances to keep client portals online.

Today, modern cloud ecosystems like Google Firebase offer permanent free tiers (Spark Plan) that include 50,000 document reads and 20,000 document writes every day. For a building with 25 rental units, daily rent updates consume less than 5% of this free quota. That means zero server bills for you, and zero hosting overhead for us.

Summary: Make the Math Work for You

Don't lease the software that manages your leases. Own your tools, keep your database in your own private cloud, and eliminate monthly recurring bills forever.
