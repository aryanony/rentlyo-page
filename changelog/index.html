Public Release History

Rentlyo Release Notes & Ledger

When you purchase software for a one-time fee, the natural question is: "Will this project be maintained?" Here is our dated, verified release log of every engine enhancement, feature rollout, and architectural upgrade.

Current Release

v1.2.0 — Multi-Unit Consolidation & Utility Engine

September 2026

Engineered for complex commercial properties, multi-room hostel agreements, and sub-meter power billing accuracy.

NEW

Multi-Unit Deal Consolidation

Commercial tenants leasing multiple contiguous shops or residential tenants holding dual flats now operate on a unified lease ledger without skewing building occupancy metrics.

NEW

Tiered Electricity Sub-Meter Calculator

Direct reading subtraction with per-unit tariff configuration. Admin enters current meter units; system calculates difference, applies commercial/domestic slab rate, and appends to tenant's current month due.

IMPROVED

In-App OTA Release Update Notification

Tenants and owners are notified directly inside the Android app when a newer build APK is published, with a one-tap update installer.

Core Update

v1.1.0 — UPI Handoff & WhatsApp Receipts

July 2026

Focused on eliminating friction in rent collection and giving tenants immediate digital payment proof without third-party gateway charges.

NEW

Direct UPI Intent Deep-Linking

Tenants tap "Pay Now" and launch directly into PhonePe, Google Pay, or Paytm with the exact rent amount and owner UPI ID pre-populated. Zero gateway fees (0%).

NEW

Instant WhatsApp Receipt Dispatch

Upon verifying bank credit, the owner taps "Confirm" in Admin Console. Rentlyo generates a clean, itemized WhatsApp payment receipt with deal balance and electricity breakdown.

SECURITY

6-Digit PIN & Biometric App Lock

Hardware-backed cryptographic storage for quick unlock. Owners and tenants can securely access their ledgers without re-entering full credentials on shared devices.

Foundation

v1.0.0 — Production General Availability

May 2026

The commercial launch of the Rentlyo dual-application suite and the white-label deployment engine.

NEW

Rentlyo Tenant Companion Android App

Native Flutter application for renters. Live dues balance, agreement duration, utility history, digital gate pass, and maintenance tickets.

NEW

Rentlyo Admin Console Android App

Unit inventory grid, 1-click tenant onboarding, step-up rent escalation scheduling, security deposit tracking, and PDF ledger exports.

INFRASTRUCTURE

Zero-Server-Cost Architecture

Runs on Google Firebase Spark (free tier) quota. Zero monthly hosting or database costs for properties managing 5 to 50 units.

Upcoming Development Roadmap

All updates are backward-compatible. Because you own the APK and your database sits on your own Firebase instance, updates are delivered cleanly without forcing unwanted design changes.

In Testing

Housing Society Maintenance Billing

Quarterly maintenance levy rules, common area electric splits, and society meeting minutes broadcast.

In Design

Interactive Web Dashboard Preview

Read-only web browser portal for desktop ledger auditing and annual tax statement generation.
