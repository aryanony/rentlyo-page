The Situation Before Rentlyo

The Paper Register Nightmare

Like thousands of property owners across India, Arya Plaza was managed on physical diary registers and informal WhatsApp threads. Managing a mix of ground-floor retail shops (clothing stores, medical clinics, offices) and upper-floor residential tenants led to predictable friction:

1

Sub-Meter Power Disputes

Every month, the owner had to manually calculate sub-meter unit differences on scratch paper. Tenants frequently questioned whether previous reading math was accurate.

2

Partial Rent Fog

Tenants paid in 2 or 3 installments (e.g. ₹8,000 cash, ₹10,000 via UPI later). Tracking what was paid, what was overdue, and advance balance required flipping back through messy paper sheets.

3

No Formal Receipts

Commercial shopkeepers required formal rent receipts for GST filing and business accounts. Creating manual handwritten receipts every month was hours of wasted administrative time.

What Was Deployed

Custom White-Label Setup in 48 Hours

Using Rentlyo's automated deployment engine (`setup_new_client.ps1`), Arya Plaza was provisioned with:

A

Arya Plaza Admin Android App

Configured for the property owner with 25 pre-seeded units, shop dimensions, meter serial numbers, and landlord UPI ID (`aryan.gupta@okaxis`).

B

Tenant Companion App with Arya Plaza Branding

Delivered via WhatsApp APK link to every shopkeeper and tenant. Allows renters to log in with phone number + 6-digit PIN and immediately view their monthly breakdown.

C

Automated Electricity Sub-Meter Tariffs

Domestic residential units configured at ₹8/unit; commercial ground-floor shops configured with their respective commercial tariffs. The owner simply enters the end-of-month meter reading and Rentlyo calculates the bill instantly.

The Verifiable Result

What Changed Operationally?

Zero Disputed Calculations

Tenants tap their app and inspect previous meter reading, current reading, units consumed, and tariff multiplier. Because the math is calculated by the software, arguments over scratchpad arithmetic stopped completely.

Faster UPI Collection Cycle

Tenants tap "Pay Now" on the 1st of the month, which deep-links directly into PhonePe/GPay. The owner receives bank confirmation and clicks "Confirm" in Admin to issue an instant WhatsApp receipt.
