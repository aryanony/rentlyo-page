Rentlyo vs. Excel & Paper Registers

Your real alternative to software isn't another expensive app — it's the notebook in your drawer or an Excel sheet on your laptop. Here is an honest look at where manual registers shine, and where they begin costing you money.

Where Excel / Paper Wins

When You Should Keep Using Excel or Paper

Spreadsheets and physical diaries are the right choice if:

  • • You have 1 to 3 units only — managing two tenants rarely creates enough friction to warrant dedicated mobile applications.
  • • Upfront cost is strictly zero — notebooks cost ₹50 and Google Sheets is free.
  • • Tenants pay simple fixed rent with no sub-meters, never pay in installments, and live in the same house.
Where Rentlyo Wins

When Excel & Registers Start Hurting Your Business

Rentlyo becomes essential the moment you cross 5 units:

  • ✓ Electricity Sub-Meter Calculations — tenants can see their previous reading, current reading, unit rate, and exact bill on their phone. Zero arguments.
  • ✓ Tenant Self-Service — tenants don't need to text you: "Uncle, kitna baaki hai?" They open their app and see the live balance 24/7.
  • ✓ Automated WhatsApp Receipts — one tap in Admin generates an itemized payment receipt sent straight to the tenant's WhatsApp.
  • ✓ Installment & Advance Clarity — handles partial payments and deposit drawdowns without broken spreadsheet formulas.

The Real Math

The Hidden Cost of "Free" Registers

Paper registers feel free, but consider the true cost for a 20-unit building over a year:

3–5 Hours / Week

Wasted calculating sub-meters, writing paper receipts, sending manual reminders, and reconciling bank SMS records.

₹10,000–₹25,000 / Year

In uncollected late fees, untracked utility shortfalls, or deposit disputes caused by messy informal paper ledgers.

Reputation & Stress

Commercial tenants respect landlords who provide professional, branded digital ledgers. Paper slips look amateur.
