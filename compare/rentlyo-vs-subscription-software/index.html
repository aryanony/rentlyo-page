Rentlyo vs. Subscription Software

Most software comparisons pretend their product wins at everything. We won't do that. Here is an honest breakdown of where recurring SaaS genuinely makes sense, and where one-time ownership wins hands down.

Where Subscription SaaS Wins

When You Should Choose Subscription SaaS

Monthly recurring platforms (like MyGate, NoBrokerHood, or ApnaComplex) are superior if:

  • • You manage 300–1,000+ units in a high-rise society requiring physical boom-barrier hardware integrations and dedicated security booth tablets.
  • • You need a 24/7 outsourced call center to answer late-night tenant intercom calls.
  • • Your corporate entity prefers operational expense (OpEx) write-offs on monthly invoices rather than a single software capital asset.
Where Rentlyo Wins

When You Should Choose Rentlyo

Rentlyo is overwhelmingly the superior investment if:

  • ✓ You manage 5 to 50 units (residential flats, commercial market shops, PG rooms, or warehouse godowns).
  • ✓ You hate perpetual rent-seeking fees — you want to pay once (₹20,000) and own your platform for life.
  • ✓ You want data privacy — your database lives in your own Google Firebase instance, never shared with third-party brokers or ad networks.
  • ✓ You need utility sub-meter calculations — direct difference math with custom tariffs built specifically for Indian properties.
