Frequently Asked Questions

Every question property buyers ask, answered transparently with zero fluff.

What happens if you stop supporting the software?

You retain your installed applications and full control over your Firebase database. Because Rentlyo operates directly on your own Firebase instance and does not route through any intermediary subscription server, your software and rent data will continue to run indefinitely without dependence on us.

Are there really no monthly server or database bills?

Yes, zero monthly server bills. Rentlyo is architected entirely on Google Firebase's generous free tier (Spark Plan), which includes 50,000 reads and 20,000 writes every day. For properties with 5 to 50 units, normal daily rent management uses less than 10% of this free quota.

Do I need technical or coding knowledge to use this?

No technical knowledge is required. We deliver pre-compiled, branded Android APKs configured with your property name, logo, and payment UPI. You and your tenants simply install the app like any standard Android app and log in with your phone number and 6-digit PIN.

Can I manage multiple properties?

Yes, Rentlyo Admin includes built-in multi-property context switching. You can switch between residential apartments, commercial shopping complexes, and PG hostels within the same admin interface without logging out.

Is my property and tenant financial data safe?

Yes, your data is protected by Google Firebase infrastructure and battle-tested Firestore security rules. Tenants can only read and modify their own specific lease deal, while owners have protected administrative access enforced by cryptographic role checks.

How does tenant rent payment work?

Tenants pay directly via UPI apps using standard deep links to your bank account. Rentlyo initiates a one-tap handoff to PhonePe, Google Pay, or Paytm, and generates an instant payment report and WhatsApp receipt for owner confirmation. Money goes straight to your bank account with zero platform commission.

How are electric sub-meters calculated?

Admins enter current meter readings and Rentlyo handles the arithmetic. The system retrieves the previous reading, calculates net consumed units, multiplies by the unit's configured tariff (e.g. ₹9.00/kWh), and automatically posts the utility charge onto the rent ledger.

Can I change my property logo or brand colors without rebuilding APKs?

Yes, Rentlyo includes Cloud Dynamic Rebranding. You can update your property logo, primary and accent theme colors, tagline, and WhatsApp contact number in your Firestore database. Both the Admin Console and Tenant Companion apps dynamically fetch and render your new theme in real-time without re-compiling or re-installing APKs.

How do in-app software updates work without Google Play Store?

Rentlyo includes an Over-The-Air (OTA) Release Publisher built into the Admin Console. You can upload new APK release packages with release notes directly to your private Firebase Storage. When tenants launch their app, they are prompted with a release update dialog and can download and install updates in-app without any Google Play Store review delays or $25 developer account fees.

Is Hindi language supported for non-English speaking tenants and staff?

Yes, Rentlyo includes 100% native bilingual localization for both English and Hindi. With a single tap in settings, every screen—including rent ledgers, notices, payment reporting, gate passes, and PIN screens—switches instantly to clean Hindi, ensuring caretakers and local tenants navigate dues effortlessly.

How do Hostel & PG features like mess menus and outpasses work?

Rentlyo includes a dedicated PG & Hostel engine. Wardens can publish weekly 4-meal daily schedules (Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Snacks, Dinner) that sync live to the student app. Students can also request digital gate passes and curfew outpasses with departure and return timestamps for warden sign-off.
