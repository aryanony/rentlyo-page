Commercial Real Estate

Built for Shopping Plazas & Commercial Complexes

Multi-unit shop consolidation, high-tariff electricity billing, and formal ledger statements. Validated daily at Arya Plaza, Munger.

Multi-Unit Deal Consolidation

When an anchor retailer leases Shop 101, 102, and 103 together, Rentlyo consolidates the contract into one unified ledger while maintaining individual unit occupancy and sub-meter records.

Commercial Sub-Meter Tariffs

Configure custom commercial power tariffs (e.g. ₹9.50 to ₹12.00 / kWh). Track heavy machinery consumption and automatically debit electricity from monthly rent invoices without manual math.

Step-Up Rent Escalation Tiers

Commercial 3-year and 5-year lease agreements mandate scheduled rent step-ups (e.g. 5% after month 12). Pre-program milestones to auto-update dues without rewriting agreements.

CA & Tax-Ready Excel Exports

Export full financial year statements with one tap. Hand clean CSV/Excel spreadsheets with complete GST, rent, and utility breakdowns directly to your Chartered Accountant.

Biometric PIN Security Lockout

Safeguard sensitive commercial rental contracts and bank cash flow records with 6-digit cryptographic PIN authentication, fingerprint unlocking, and brute-force lockout safeguards.

OTA In-App Release Publisher

Push software patches, maintenance updates, and new features directly to commercial tenants over the air from the Admin Console. Completely bypass Google Play Store review delays.
